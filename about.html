🌐 About Our Project

Responsible Netizens – Need of the Hour is a Digital Awareness Project created to spread awareness about safe, responsible and respectful use of the internet.

The internet has become an important part of our everyday lives. Along with its benefits, it also brings challenges such as fake news, phishing, privacy risks and irresponsible social media use.

Through this website, we aim to educate users about these digital challenges and encourage them to Think, Verify, Protect, Respect and Report.

💡 Why This Project?

Every day, people use smartphones, social media, websites and online services. However, not everyone is aware of the risks associated with the digital world.

Our project aims to make digital safety simple, understandable and interactive for everyone.
